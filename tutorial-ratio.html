Tutorial: text with your own font (RATIO B)

Draw a small digit font, then show two lines of text that one knob drives, like an FM synth's RATIO B. Its knob has 361 detents: the top line picks one of 19 ratios every 19 detents, and the bottom line counts through all 19 in between, like a watch's hands. About 20 minutes. Fonts work the same for letters: step 7.

Inspired by the Elektron DN's screens. Not affiliated with Elektron.

0 Why text, not pictures

There are 361 different screens here. As frames that would be 361 pictures: about 7.9 KB, and more than a frames element allows (255). As text it's 11 glyphs (0–9 and ".") and 19 short strings, about 200 bytes. Any number of combinations then costs nothing extra.

Two pieces, each with its own page:

  • Font editor: draw the glyphs, check that the strings fit.
  • Layout preview: two value elements that use the font, and an index that splits one knob into two lists.

Load this result puts a step's result into the font editor or the layout preview (they open in their own tabs and pick it up).

1 A new font

  1. Open the font editor. Click New font… and name it ratio.
  2. Set Height 5, Spacing 1 (one blank column between characters).

2 Draw 0–9 and the point

  1. Type 0 (outside any text box) to jump to it. Click + until it's 3 wide, then paint it. Click or drag to paint; starting on a lit pixel erases.
  2. Do the same for 1 to 9, all 3 wide. Copy / Paste saves time: 3 is 2 with a new bottom, and 6 and 9 start from 8. Keep the corners round, like the 0.
  3. The . is 1 wide: a single pixel on the bottom row.

Watch Preview on the right as you go: type 0.25 16.0 into it.

What it says:

  
    
    
  • Both elements are lists of the same 19 strings, in your font, right-aligned in 13 pixels, driven by P4.
    • 
    
  • "detents": 361 spreads the knob's range over 361 steps, end to end, rounding to the nearest. That's the same maths Sidecar's detented knobs use.
    • 
    
  • "div": 19 makes the top line move once every 19 detents.
    • 
    
  • "mod": 19 makes the bottom line count 0…18 and wrap.

5 Turn the knob

  
Because the layout uses detents, the preview's P4 slider steps through all 361 of them. Drag it: the bottom line counts up through the ratios, and every 19 steps the top line moves on, from 0.25/0.25 to 16.0/16.0.

  


  
6 Letters too

  
A font can have any printable ASCII character, upper and lower case, each as wide as you draw it:

  
    
    
  • Start from the Sidecar 3×5 or 5×7 font (A–Z, 0–9, punctuation) with trim to proportional on: an I becomes 1 wide, an M stays 5. Then add lower case.
    • 
    
  • Leaving out a letter's lower case is fine: lower-case text then uses the capital (and the other way round).
    • 
    
  • Use it for names, headers or list values, e.g. { "type": "value", "p": 1, "fmt": "list", "items": ["Sine", "Saw", "Square"], "font": "names" }.
    • 
    
  • Fit in under Strings to check catches names that are too wide for their box before they reach the Rytm.
    •
