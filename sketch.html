    Picking a size
    • Most sketches show one knob: 8 slots, 4 per row, each 32 px wide. Keep about 2 px clear each side, so 28 px wide at most.
    • Height depends on how the page splits its rows: a tall top row gives 21 px, its short bottom row 11 px, even rows 18 px each. The label goes under it.
    • Use odd sizes for things with a centre (dials, rings, a needle): 21×21 has a middle pixel, 20×20 doesn't.
    • 1 px lines and small curves only read from about 13 px; below that, draw pixel by pixel.
    • Leave text out: labels and values belong to the layout, which draws them in its fonts.
